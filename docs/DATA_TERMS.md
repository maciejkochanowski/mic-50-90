# Source-data terms for MIC-50-90 1.0.0

The software uses MIT. Example inputs retain their own source terms and attribution. The software package contains small runnable examples and source records, not the complete research data collection.

- Keep the source DOI or agency attribution, original denominator, panel and rank assumptions with each example.
- The Delma et al. inputs carry the source article's CC BY-NC 4.0 terms; see `examples/published_table/SOURCE.json` and `examples/returned_counts/SOURCE.json`.
- Other publication-derived inputs retain source-specific terms. No article, publisher figure, full-text XML or complete source table is bundled.
- Selected NARMS/CDC aggregate examples retain federal source attribution. NCBI hosting alone does not establish a licence for every submitted record.
- Constructed calibration and decision inputs are demonstrations, not empirical validation datasets.
- Raw ATLAS workbooks and derived histogram tables, full surveillance workbooks and historical campaign results are not supplied.

[Data sources](../data/SOURCES.md) maps the examples to their provenance records; [data licences](../data/LICENSES.md) records the separate rights. Font licences remain beside the bundled font files.
